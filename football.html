Football Programme

Building confidence, skill and teamwork through fun football training for all ages.

About Our Football Programme

Our football training is designed for children aged 6–15, focusing on confidence-building, physical development, and teamwork. Whether your child is a beginner or already playing in a club, we provide a supportive space to grow.

What Children Learn

  • Dribbling, passing & ball control
  • Team play & communication
  • Match tactics & positioning
  • Speed, agility & fitness
  • Confidence & discipline

Weekly Sessions

⚽ Junior Group (Ages 6–10)
Beginner-friendly sessions with fun games and small matches.

⚽ Youth Group (Ages 11–15)
More advanced drills, competitive play and development-focused training.
