Cricket Programme

Soft-ball cricket for beginner to intermediate players of all ages.

About Our Cricket Training

Our cricket programme focuses on fun, participation and skills development. Children learn how to bat, bowl, field and work as a team.

Skills We Develop

  • Batting technique
  • Bowling accuracy
  • Catching & fielding
  • Reflexes & timing
  • Team coordination

Weekly Sessions

🏏 Soft-ball mini games
🏏 Skill-based circuits
🏏 Friendly community matches
