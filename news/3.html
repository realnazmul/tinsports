Families Enjoy Sunshine at Community Cricket Day

Community • City Park Field • July 2025

Our annual Community Cricket Day brought families together for a warm summer afternoon of soft-ball cricket, games, and outdoor fun. Over 150 parents and children attended, making it one of our largest cricket gatherings yet!

Fun for All Ages

Young players enjoyed cricket mini-games including bowling accuracy, target batting, relay catching drills, and soft-ball family matches. Even parents joined in — some for the first time!

Festival Atmosphere

Alongside the cricketing action, families enjoyed:

  • 🍧 Ice cream & drink stalls
  • 🎉 Face-painting for kids
  • 📸 Photo booth with props
  • 🏏 Mini cricket challenges with prizes

Coaches’ Praise

Our coaching team highlighted the excellent enthusiasm from every participant. Many children showed significant improvement in hand–eye coordination and confidence.

“The positive energy today was amazing. Kids supported each other, learned new skills, and most importantly — had fun!”
— Coach Daniel

Thank you to everyone who joined us. We look forward to hosting more community events to bring families together through sport!
