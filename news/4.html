Friendly Volleyball Day

Friendly • Community Sports Hall • June 2025

A fantastic group of young athletes joined our Friendly Volleyball Day, a relaxed but exciting afternoon of rallies, teamwork, and fun competitions. Participants from ages 8–15 came together to experience the joy of volleyball in a supportive environment.

Warm-Up & Skill Building

Coaches started the day with movement games, simple passing drills, and partner rallies. Many players were completely new to volleyball — and they picked up the basics incredibly quickly!

Team Play & Match Moments

Mixed-age teams played a series of friendly sets, where communication and encouragement were the key highlights. The atmosphere was full of positivity as every point earned cheers from teammates and parents.

Highlights of the Day

  • 🏐 Longest rally lasted 32 touches!
  • 👏 Great sportsmanship from every player
  • 🎉 Fun games like “Serve & Sprint” and “Target Zone”
  • 📸 Family photo moments and medal distribution

A Growing Sport in Our Community

More young players are showing interest in volleyball, and this event marked a big step toward expanding the programme. It was a proud moment seeing friendships forming and confidence growing on the court.

Thank you to all coaches, parents, and athletes who joined us. More volleyball events will be coming soon!
