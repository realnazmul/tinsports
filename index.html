About T.I.N. Sports

We’re a welcoming children’s sports community, helping young people grow through active play, teamwork, and confidence-building coaching.

🌟

Inclusive

Everyone’s welcome — all ages, all abilities.

😊

Fun First

Our sessions are playful, energetic, and always safe.

🤝

Teamwork

We learn to support and encourage each other on and off the pitch.

🎯

Skills

Our coaches focus on developing balance, coordination, and confidence.

Our Story

We began as a small team of volunteers and have grown into a lively community hub for parents and children who share a love of sport, friendship, and healthy living.

Membership Plans

Pick a plan that suits your family. All memberships include access to facilities and certified coaching.

Annual cost £50

Everyone aged 5–20 is welcome to join our sessions for just £50 anually. Play any sport you like. To join, you can pay in person or contact us on WhatsApp to pay online.
